A scheduled action books the finished periods of all accounts every night.
Opening an account or printing a statement computes it at once.

- *Attendances → My Working Time Account*: every employee sees their own
  entries and balance.
- *Attendances → Working Time Account → Accounts*: all accounts with balance,
  the number of periods to clarify and the warnings. Attendance officers see
  the accounts of their employees.
- *Entries*: all entries. Periods *to clarify* have scheduled days without
  attendance and without time off. Enter the missing time off, or mark the
  entries and choose *Confirm Minus Hours*. *Not Booked Because* on an entry
  books the period with zero.
- *Book by Hand*: opening balance, correction, payout or forfeiture, with a
  reason. Payouts and forfeitures are entered with negative hours. A wrong
  entry is reversed with *Reverse* and booked again.
- *Statements and Records*: the account statement or the record of working
  time for one or several employees and a period, as PDF.
- *Close Month*: closes all accounts of the company up to a date. Periods to
  clarify have to be settled first. *Correct Closed Periods* books what
  changed in closed periods afterwards as corrections dated today.

Only administrators of the attendances book by hand, confirm minus hours and
close months.
