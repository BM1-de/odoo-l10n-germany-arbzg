A signed working time account (*Arbeitszeitkonto*) for the attendances
of Odoo: for every day, week or month it compares the worked hours to
the hours of the working schedule and books the difference as plus or
minus hours. The balance of an employee is the sum of all entries and
can be negative.

Odoo 19 kept such a balance as overtime. Odoo 20 replaced it by time
rules that split attendances and add lines for missing time, without a
balance that can go below zero. This module brings the account back and
keeps the attendances untouched: it only reads them. Begin, end and
duration of the working time stay exactly as recorded, which is what
German law asks employers to record (BAG, 13 September 2022, 1 ABR
22/21; § 17 MiLoG for marginal employment).

- **Computation:** worked hours come from the validated attendances,
  expected hours from the working schedule of the contract version valid
  on each day. Public holidays and time off that counts as worked
  (vacation, sickness) lower the expected hours, so they leave the
  account as it is.
- **Tolerance:** differences within a tolerance are booked with zero,
  beyond it the whole difference is booked.
- **Minus hours** are only booked when the contract version says so, as
  they need an agreement. A period can be set to zero with a reason, for
  example when the employer had no work for the employee.
- **Days without attendance** and without time off are not booked as
  minus right away. They wait as *to clarify* until the time off is
  entered or the minus hours are confirmed.
- **Compensation by time off:** time off of a type marked as *deducted
  from the working time account* keeps the expected hours, so the day is
  booked as minus.
- **Entries by hand:** opening balance, correction, payout and
  forfeiture, each with a reason. Entries are never deleted, they are
  reversed.
- **Closing:** closed months are not computed again. Later changes of
  attendances are booked as a correction in an open period.
- **§ 2 (2) MiLoG:** for accounts under the German Minimum Wage Act the
  module warns when plus hours get twelve months old and when a month
  adds more than half of the agreed monthly working time.
- **Documents:** an account statement and a record of working time
  (begin, end and duration per day) per employee as PDF, for the payroll
  records.
- **Coming from Odoo 19:** the overtime balance of Odoo 19 is taken over
  as it was, entry by entry.

The module is no payroll, no long-term account (*Wertguthaben*, § 7b SGB
IV) and no legal advice. It is provided without warranty; whether an
account with minus hours is allowed depends on the agreements of the
employer.

Configuration
=============

*Attendances → Configuration → Settings*, block *Working Time Account
(Germany)*: the defaults for new contract versions (period, tolerances,
minus hours).

On the employee, in the work information next to the working schedule
(the values belong to the contract version, so they can change over
time):

- *Working Time Account*: per day, per week (Monday to Sunday) or per
  month. A schedule without fixed days is compared per week at least.
- *Tolerance for Plus Hours* and *Tolerance for Minus Hours*.
- *Book Minus Hours*: only with an agreement with the employee.
- *Monitor § 2 (2) MiLoG*: for accounts under the Minimum Wage Act. It
  does not apply when the steady monthly pay already covers the minimum
  wage for all hours; the module cannot tell, as it does not know wages.
- *Working Time Account Since*: nothing is computed before this date.
  Left empty, the account starts the day it is computed for the first
  time.

On the types of time off (*Time Off → Configuration → Time Off Types*):
*Deducted from the Working Time Account* for time off that compensates
plus hours. Leave it off for vacation, sickness and everything else that
counts as worked.

An opening balance is booked under *Attendances → Working Time Account →
Book by Hand*.

Coming from Odoo 19: *Attendances → Configuration → Take Over from Odoo
19* takes over the overtime entries up to a date and, if wanted, the
period and tolerances of the overtime rulesets. Take the end of a period
as the date: a Sunday for weekly accounts, the last day of a month for
monthly ones. *Compare with the Computation* lists the periods the
module would compute differently, without storing anything.

Usage
=====

A scheduled action books the finished periods of all accounts every
night. Opening an account or printing a statement computes it at once.

- *Attendances → My Working Time Account*: every employee sees their own
  entries and balance.
- *Attendances → Working Time Account → Accounts*: all accounts with
  balance, the number of periods to clarify and the warnings. Attendance
  officers see the accounts of their employees.
- *Entries*: all entries. Periods *to clarify* have scheduled days
  without attendance and without time off. Enter the missing time off,
  or mark the entries and choose *Confirm Minus Hours*. *Not Booked
  Because* on an entry books the period with zero.
- *Book by Hand*: opening balance, correction, payout or forfeiture,
  with a reason. Payouts and forfeitures are entered with negative
  hours. A wrong entry is reversed with *Reverse* and booked again.
- *Statements and Records*: the account statement or the record of
  working time for one or several employees and a period, as PDF.
- *Close Month*: closes all accounts of the company up to a date.
  Periods to clarify have to be settled first. *Correct Closed Periods*
  books what changed in closed periods afterwards as corrections dated
  today.

Only administrators of the attendances book by hand, confirm minus hours
and close months.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- The module does not know wages. It cannot check the earnings limit of
  marginal employment, nor whether the steady pay already covers the
  minimum wage (the exception of § 2 (2) MiLoG).
- Schedules without fixed days: Odoo spreads the weekly hours over the
  first days of the period asked for. A public holiday therefore lowers
  the expected hours of the week only if it falls on one of these days.
- An attendance belongs to the day it started on. Work over midnight is
  not split.
- Limits for the balance (traffic light) and automatic forfeiture are
  not built in yet; forfeitures are booked by hand.
- Employees see their entries, but only attendance officers print
  statements.
- The account is computed once a night, not at the moment an attendance
  changes.
- Not covered: surcharges, payroll export, long-term accounts (§ 7b SGB
  IV), collective agreements with their own rules.
