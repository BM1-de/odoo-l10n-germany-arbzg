The German Working Time Act in your attendances

Legal breaks inserted or pointed out, the 10-hour limit reported, forgotten check-outs ended at the maximum

What it checks

  • Breaks (§ 4 ArbZG): more than 6 hours of work a day need 30 minutes of break, more than 9 hours 45 minutes. Gaps of at least 15 minutes count, and so do the breaks of the working schedule that Odoo deducts.
  • Only the time above a limit needs a break: 6:10 hours of work need 10 minutes.
  • A missing break is put into the middle of the longest attendance of the day and noted there, or only pointed out to the employee.
  • Maximum (§ 3 ArbZG): more than 10 hours of work a day are reported to the employee and to a responsible person, once per day. The attendances are not shortened, as they must show the real time (§ 16 (2) ArbZG).
  • Forgotten check-out: every 15 minutes, open attendances that reached the maximum of the day are checked out.
  • Every correction checks the whole day again. Contractors, freelancers and exempt employees such as managing directors are not checked.

Good to know

  • Employees should still record their breaks by checking out and in; the rule is only a safety net.
  • Only the total of the breaks of a day is checked, not the six hours of work in a row of § 4 sentence 3 ArbZG. Not checked: the daily rest period, Sundays and public holidays, night work, the weekly working time, young employees and collective agreements.
