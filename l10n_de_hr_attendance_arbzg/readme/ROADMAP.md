- Only the total of the breaks of a day is checked, not the six hours of work
  in a row of § 4 sentence 3 ArbZG.
- Not checked: the daily rest period (§ 5), Sundays and public holidays
  (§§ 9–11), night work (§ 6), the weekly working time, young employees
  (JArbSchG) and collective agreements (§ 7).
- Odoo 20 no longer deducts the lunch break of the working schedule from the
  worked hours. An attendance from 8:00 to 17:00 is 9 hours of work there, and
  the break is inserted, where Odoo 19 counted the hour of the schedule.
- *Break Management on Checkout* of Odoo 20 asks for the break after the
  check-out. With *Insert the missing break*, the legal break is inserted at
  the check-out already, and a break entered afterwards is deducted in
  addition; use *Only point out the missing break* together with it.
- Odoo does not take back the lines its time rules have split off. If a break
  is inserted later into a day that already has such lines, because the day
  was corrected, they stay as they are.
